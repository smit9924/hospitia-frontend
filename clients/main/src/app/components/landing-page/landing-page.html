About Yatri Bhavan

Building community, one guest at a time

Our Mission

Yatri Bhavan is an NGO-run hotel dedicated to providing affordable, quality accommodation while supporting local communities. "Yatri Bhavan" means "Traveler's Home" in Sanskrit, and that's exactly what we aim to be for every guest.

Our Values

  • Affordability: Quality stays at fair prices
  • Community Service: Supporting local development
  • Hospitality: Genuine warmth and care for guests
  • Sustainability: Eco-friendly practices

Your stay makes an impact! A portion of every booking goes directly to supporting education and healthcare initiatives in underserved communities.
